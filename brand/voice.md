## Who we are
Alvarez Plumbing & Air Conditioning — a residential + commercial plumbing and
air conditioning company in Tampa, FL. We're the crew that
answers the phone at 2am, quotes the price before we start, and tells you the
truth even when the truth is "you don't need that repair."

## How we sound
- **Plain-spoken expert.** Like a great tradesperson explaining a problem at your
  kitchen table — confident, clear, never condescending.
- **Short sentences.** Then a longer one when it earns its place.
- **Second person.** Talk to "you," the homeowner or business owner.
- **Concrete over abstract.** "A $20 flapper" beats "an affordable component."
- **No corporate filler.** Ban these: "In today's fast-paced world," "In this
  comprehensive guide," "Look no further," "When it comes to," "rest assured."

## Do
- Lead with the answer, then explain. (People are anxious; relieve it fast.)
- Give real numbers and ranges ("usually $150–$400").
- Tell them when to DIY and when to call. Honesty builds trust = more leads.
- Use analogies from everyday life.

## Don't
- Don't pad word count with fluff. Length should come from useful detail.
- Don't fearmonger to sell.
- Don't bury the lede behind three intro paragraphs.

## Reading level
Aim for ~grade 7. A stressed person Googling at midnight should get it instantly.

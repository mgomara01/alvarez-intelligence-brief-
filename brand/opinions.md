# Opinions & Hot Takes

> Opinions make content yours. Generic content has none, which is exactly why it
> reads like everyone else's. Take real, defensible stances. Replace these with
> what YOU actually believe.

- **Tankless isn't always worth it.** For small households with low hot-water
  use, a good tank heater is the smarter buy. We'll say so even though tankless
  has a bigger ticket.
- **"Whole-home" anything is usually an upsell.** Most homes need a fix, not a
  system overhaul. We size the solution to the problem.
- **Drain chemicals are a trap.** They eat pipes and rarely fix the real clog.
  A snake or a camera beats a bottle of caustic goo every time.
- **The cheapest quote is usually the most expensive.** Lowball quotes leave out
  the parts that matter, then "discover" them mid-job.
- **Maintenance > emergencies.** A $150 annual tune-up prevents the $4,000 winter
  breakdown. Boring, unglamorous, true.
- **Permits are not optional.** Unpermitted work tanks home resale and insurance
  claims. We pull them. Period.

## How to use opinions
Drop ONE relevant opinion into a post where it naturally fits — usually when
comparing options or warning about a common mistake. Don't force all of them in.

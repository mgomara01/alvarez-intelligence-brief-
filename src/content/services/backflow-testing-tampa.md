## What backflow testing covers

A backflow preventer stops contaminated water from being siphoned back into the clean supply — protecting your drinking water (and everyone else's). The annual test confirms the assembly still seals correctly. We:

- Test the assembly and certify the result
- File the report directly with your Tampa Bay water authority
- Repair or rebuild a failed assembly and retest
- Track your due date so next year is automatic
